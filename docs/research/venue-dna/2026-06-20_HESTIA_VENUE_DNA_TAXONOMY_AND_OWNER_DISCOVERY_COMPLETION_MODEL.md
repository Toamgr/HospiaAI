# HESTIA Venue DNA Taxonomy & Owner Discovery Completion Model  
## Executive Thesis  
A hospitality venue is a complex operational and experiential ecosystem rather than a simple retail transaction space. Standard business intelligence systems often fail because they focus on retrospective financial transactions, ignoring the qualitative factors that drive premium hospitality. Conversely, traditional brand agencies create conceptual guidelines that struggle to translate into daily front-of-house behaviors. To bridge this gap, the HESTIA platform establishes a unified framework called "Venue DNA." This framework integrates qualitative founder intentions with physical environments, service standards, and local market realities.

Venue DNA is the core operational blueprint that connects a founder's vision with daily guest experiences. It comprises eight key pillars:  
* **Identity**: The venue's category, capacity, and spatial limitations.  
* **Emotional Promise**: The specific feeling and mood the space is designed to evoke.  
* **Physical World**: Spatial design, acoustics, lighting transitions, and flow.  
* **Guest Fit**: Matching target guest profiles with local neighborhood demographics.  
* **Operational Behavior**: Daily service standards and shift execution.  
* **Service Philosophy**: The venue's foundational perspective on guest care and interaction.  
* **Commercial Logic**: Revenue goals, ticket averages, and product margins.  
* **Founder Belief**: The core values, boundaries, and motivations of the owner.  
The HESTIA Owner AI functions as an active-listening, schema-guided discovery system. Instead of relying on static, repetitive forms that cause user fatigue, the platform parses natural conversation to map the venue's DNA in real time. It tracks completed, partial, and missing data points, asking targeted questions only when necessary to produce a validated Working Venue DNA Brief.  
## Venue DNA Is Not Just Brand  
To build a reliable operating intelligence engine, the system must separate superficial brand styling from the functional DNA of the business. Brand guidelines often focus on visual assets like logos, color palettes, and copywriting styles. While these elements shape external marketing, they do not dictate how a team handles a busy Friday night shift or manages tableside service.

**Complete Venue DNA Taxonomy**  
The HESTIA database organizes the venue's profile into 35 distinct dimensions. This structure ensures that every extracted detail is stored correctly and made available to the appropriate downstream modules.  
## 1. Venue Identity  
* **Definition:** The foundational category, operational format, scale, and layout structure of the business.  
* **Why It Matters:** Defines the core constraints and templates used across all HESTIA operational modules.  
* **Example Owner Answers:** "A cozy, high-craft corner cocktail bar with 60 seats."  
* **State Classification Criteria:**  
    * *Answered:* Explicitly stated format, maximum capacity, and primary focus.  
    * *Partial:* Format mentioned without capacity metrics or operational scale.  
    * *Missing:* Ambiguity regarding the business format or scale.  
    * *Requires Confirmation:* Exact spatial limits and capacity zones.  
* **Dependent HESTIA Modules:** Core Operating Engine, Guest Intelligence.  
## 2. Founder / Owner Intent  
* **Definition:** The underlying personal and professional motivations driving the owner's investment.  
* **Why It Matters:** Guides how HESTIA frames its recommendations, balancing long-term growth with immediate profit goals.  
* **Example Owner Answers:** "To build a neighborhood staple that introduces high-end mixology to an underserved local crowd."  
* **State Classification Criteria:**  
    * *Answered:* Clear statement of personal success and brand goals.  
    * *Partial:* Simple financial goals without personal or qualitative context.  
    * *Missing:* No personal connection or motivation recorded.  
    * *Requires Confirmation:* Short-term exit goals versus long-term brand legacy.  
* **Dependent HESTIA Modules:** Brand Strategy, Academy / Training.  
## 3. Business Purpose  
* **Definition:** The core market thesis, detailing the specific problem the venue solves for its local audience.

    * *Requires Confirmation:* Check targets and volume-to-value expectations.  
* **Dependent HESTIA Modules:** Brand / Positioning, Marketing.

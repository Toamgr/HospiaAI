# HESTIA Intelligence Doctrine v1 — Archived Research Draft (NON-CANONICAL)

> The canonical doctrine is [docs/architecture/HESTIA_INTELLIGENCE_DOCTRINE_V1.md](../../../architecture/HESTIA_INTELLIGENCE_DOCTRINE_V1.md). This document is supporting research only — see the banner above.

### 1. Core Product Thesis

HESTIA is a **Hospitality Intelligence Operating System (HOS)** designed as the "hearth" of the venue—the central operational fire around which a service team gathers 1. It acts as a comprehensive intelligence layer above transactional tools (POS, PMS, scheduling), converting fragmented daily activity into a compounding institutional asset 2. HESTIA’s fundamental purpose is to preserve venue identity and reduce management fatigue by ensuring that what is learned on a Tuesday is remembered on a Saturday, maintaining the venue’s "Living Room" standards through deterministic intelligence and structured memory 2-4.

### 2. What HESTIA Is / Is Not

* **HESTIA IS:** An operational control plane, a memory engine, and an expert hospitality reasoning system embedded in specific workflows 2. It is the "Intelligence Layer" of the venue that connects emotional context with commercial rigor 5.
* **HESTIA IS NOT:** A generic analytics dashboard, a POS clone, a chatbot, a lead-gen CRM, or a generic task manager for software teams 2, 6. It is explicitly not a "nice to have" reporting tool, but a daily operating habit engine 7.

### 3. The Intelligence Layers

HESTIA functions as a single venue-aware brain with integrated layers:

* **Venue DNA:** The strategic root that defines founder intent, brand promise, and non-negotiables 8-10.
* **Emotional Hospitality Context:** Reasoning through atmosphere, guest experience, and the "Living Room" feel 11, 12.
* **Physical Venue Atmosphere:** Understanding spatial geometries and sensory choreography (lighting, sound, temperature) 13, 14.
* **F&B Intelligence:** Mathematical and creative engine for menu design and performance 9, 15.
* **Beverage Intelligence:** Expert vertical logic for cocktail, wine, and spirit programs 15.
* **Event Intelligence:** Deterministic coordination and planning through the Zohar and Design Brief engines 16.
* **Academy / Training Intelligence:** Professional formation based on exposure, drills, and verification 17, 18.
* **Service Briefing Intelligence:** Turning daily memory and feedback into team alignment 19, 20.
* **Revenue & Upsales Intelligence:** Commercial optimization based on unit economics and guest psychology 21-23.
* **Owner Intelligence:** Trend-level signals and financial risk reporting derived from real data 6, 9, 24.
* **Operational Memory:** The append-only substrate ensuring the venue learns over time 24-26.

## 4. Venue DNA & Emotional Hospitality Context

HESTIA understands a venue as a living sociotechnical system: a business, a team, a space, a service culture, and a promise to guests. Identity is not setup data. It is earned through evidence, memory, contradiction, and repeated operational behavior.

### 4.1 Venue Memory → Venue Intelligence → Venue DNA

HESTIA’s intelligence chain is built in three layers:

**Venue Memory** is the raw institutional record: founder conversations, menus, shift notes, incidents, event briefs, guest feedback, staff workarounds, training activity, pricing decisions, and operational exceptions.

**Venue Intelligence** is the synthesized interpretation of that memory. It detects patterns, contradictions, confidence gaps, behavioral signals, and drift between what the venue says it is and how it actually operates.

**Venue DNA** is the crystallized understanding of the venue reached only when HESTIA has sufficient confidence across key domains. It is not a static document. It is a living model with stable and evolving layers.

### 4.2 Uncertainty Reduction

Discovery in HESTIA is not form completion. It is uncertainty reduction.

Every question HESTIA asks should close a gap in the venue model. The system tracks what it knows, what it assumes, what is contradicted, and what remains unknown. When evidence is strong, HESTIA increases confidence. When evidence is sparse, stale, or conflicting, HESTIA should hesitate, ask better questions, or mark the domain as unresolved.

This allows HESTIA to behave less like a chatbot and more like a hospitality intelligence system that learns the venue over time.

### 4.3 Founder Digital Twin

HESTIA preserves a Founder Digital Twin: a structured understanding of the founder’s intent, standards, fears, ambitions, and non-negotiables.

This model separates two layers:

**Stable DNA Layer**
The origin story, founding vision, values, brand promise, non-negotiables, and the “Hearth”: the standard the venue would rather protect than dilute.

**Evolving Layer**
Current priorities, fears, commercial pressures, ambitions, sensitivities, and strategic focus areas. These update as new evidence accumulates from conversations, reports, decisions, events, and operational behavior.

The Founder Digital Twin is not used to imitate the founder. It is used to protect the venue from identity drift.

### 4.4 Emotional Hospitality Context

HESTIA evaluates the venue as a Unified Hospitality Organism: a space, team, menu, rhythm, and emotional contract working together to shape how guests feel.

This includes:

**Spatial Geometries**
Layout, seating density, sightlines, guest flow, intimacy zones, crowding pressure, and transition points.

**Sensory Choreography**
Lighting temperature, sound levels, acoustic transitions, music energy, table distance, material warmth, and daypart-specific atmosphere.

**Person-in-Occasion Intelligence**
HESTIA does not only ask “who is the guest?” It asks “who is this person in this occasion?”
A business lunch, luxury wedding, first date, investor dinner, family celebration, and executive meeting require different emotional contracts.

### 4.5 Atmosphere as Operational Data

Atmosphere is not decoration. In HESTIA, atmosphere is operational data.

Lighting, sound, density, table pacing, materiality, service tone, menu weight, and staff presence all influence the guest’s emotional state. HESTIA should treat these signals as part of the operating system of the venue.

Service briefs, event briefs, F&B recommendations, and owner insights should therefore include atmospheric calibration when relevant.

Examples:

* Lighting targets by daypart.
* Volume and energy curves.
* Density pressure warnings.
* Service tone adjustments.
* Event atmosphere alignment.
* Mismatch alerts between concept and execution.

### 4.6 Identity Drift Detection

HESTIA acts as the brand’s immune system.

It detects when operational reality begins to contradict the venue’s crystallized DNA. This may appear through language shifts, repeated manager notes, menu changes, cost-driven decisions, guest complaints, staff shortcuts, or service behaviors that slowly pull the venue away from its original promise.

For example, if founder language emphasizes generosity, intimacy, and ritual, but recent operational notes increasingly emphasize speed, cost-cutting, and table turnover, HESTIA should surface this as a potential identity drift signal.

The purpose is not to block change. The purpose is to make change conscious.

### 4.7 Operational Climate

HESTIA tracks Service Climate Strength: the degree to which the team shares a common understanding of standards.

A venue can have a strong brand promise but weak service climate if staff members interpret standards differently. HESTIA should detect early signs of service degradation through repeated confusion, inconsistent manager notes, training gaps, unresolved incidents, and declining staff consensus.

This allows HESTIA to warn the owner before the guest experience breaks.

### 5. Operational Memory Doctrine

Operational memory is HESTIA’s core moat 26. Every action is recorded in an **append-only event store** to preserve sequence, causality, and auditability 26, 30.

* **Raw Accumulation:** All founder statements, event outcomes, and shift observations are logged 9, 24, 31.
* **Synthesized Interpretation:** Patterns are detected across shifts (e.g., recurring bottlenecks or shortages) 28, 32.
* **Compounding Asset:** Validated patterns are promoted to Venue DNA to protect against identity drift 24.

### 6. Commercial / F&B / Beverage Intelligence Doctrine

HESTIA treats every menu item as a financial and operational unit, grounding creativity in mathematical realism 15, 33.

* **Unit Economics:** Prioritizing **Contribution Margin** (actual cash value) over simple Pour Cost percentages 21, 34.
* **Operational Realism:** Every recipe is evaluated against an **Operational Feasibility Score (OFS)**—weighting prep complexity, service speed, and staff skill 15, 35, 36.
* **Commercial Logic:** Utilizing price anchoring, sensory talking points, and cross-utilization of ingredients to maximize margin 22, 37-39.

### 7. Academy & Training Doctrine

The Academy is a **workplace capability system**, not a content platform 17, 18.

* **Training Exposure:** Lesson completion is a confirmed fact of history but indicates **exposure only**, not capability 17, 18.
* **Practical Loop:** Learning → Short In-Venue Drill → Manager/Bar Practice → Transition into real work 18, 40.
* **Verified Capability:** Skill status is only established through manual **manager verification** or direct **operational evidence** from service (e.g., resolving an incident) 28, 41.

### 8. Service Briefing Doctrine

Briefings are **exception-based** and driven by the **Shift Brain** 42, 43.

* **Contextual Alignment:** Managers receive a prioritized brief of open tasks, tonight's events, and critical items resolved in the previous shift 19, 20.
* **Inheritance:** Unresolved items from one shift are automatically carried forward, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks 3, 44.
* **Real Data:** In the absence of risks, the brief reinforces service standards rather than displaying fake "intelligence" 45.

### 9. Owner Intelligence Doctrine

Owners receive an **Operational Pulse**—a single-screen view of shift health, risk signals, and identity drift 46-48.

* **Exception-Based:** Owners only see what changed, what matters, and what needs action now 43.
* **No Faking:** The owner sees exactly what the manager sees, grounded in real operational facts 49.

### 10. Epistemic Hierarchy & Guardrails

To prevent "fake intelligence" and protect trust, every claim must carry a provenance label 50-52:

* **Known from Data (Confirmed Fact):** Directly observed (e.g., verified invoice price, timestamped RSVP) 51, 53.
* **Carefully Inferred (Observation/Inference):** Patterns noted across multiple sources or logical conclusions drawn from facts 51, 53.
* **Manager Confirmation Required:** AI-suggested incident classifications, severity scores, and staff "Ready for Task" signals 41, 52.
* **Owner Confirmation Required:** Mutations to Venue DNA, Founder Vision, or long-term strategic shifts 10, 54.
* **NEVER Claimed (Honesty Rails):**
* No fake KPIs or profit numbers without source-backed data 55, 56.
* No staff grades or personality judgments 18.
* No employee emotion analysis 29.
* No guest emotion diagnosis 57.
* No automatic Venue DNA mutation 54.

### 11. Practical Output Library

* **Pre-Shift Brief:** Top 5 risks, acknowledging today’s events and carry-forward tasks 20.
* **Upsales Brief:** Sensory talking points for "Puzzle" items and "Event-to-Profile" wine matches 22, 39.
* **Event Brief:** Zohar-generated departmental briefs (Bar, Kitchen, Floor) and Design Briefs 16, 58.
* **F&B Recommendation:** Menu engineering updates based on popularity vs. margin 59, 60.
* **Beverage Recommendation:** "Rush-hour suitable" menu tweaks and costing alerts 15, 61, 62.
* **Review-to-Action Insight:** Service failures converted into specific briefing or training points 63.
* **Academy / Training Prompt:** Drill assignments for staff based on recent operational performance 18.
* **Owner Pulse:** Exception-based summary of risk signals and identity drift 48, 64.

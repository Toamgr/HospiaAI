This folder is a research archive for HESTIA Venue Intelligence. It is not the canonical HESTIA product doctrine.

The canonical doctrine lives at:

docs/architecture/HESTIA_INTELLIGENCE_DOCTRINE_V1.md

These files support research into Venue Intelligence, Venue Memory, Venue DNA, Founder Intent, Guest Intelligence, Service Intelligence, Organizational Memory, and Decision Intelligence.

Research language in this folder may be exploratory. It should not be treated as production instruction, product-facing UI language, or implementation authority without additional review.

Any AI automation idea from this archive must pass HESTIA guardrails before implementation:

- provenance
- confidence
- role access
- venue boundaries
- human approval
- no fake intelligence

Use this archive as evidence and context. Do not use it to bypass the HESTIA Skill Pack, the canonical HESTIA Intelligence Doctrine, or the project rules for memory, provenance, and role-safe operational intelligence.

# Research: Category Discovery

Investigations into what HESTIA is at its most valuable, most defensible, and most transformative.

The central open question this folder investigates:

> *What is the most valuable layer of hospitality expertise that current software fails to capture, preserve, compound, or distribute?*

**Do not assume the answer in advance.** This folder exists to hold the investigation open, not to confirm a predetermined conclusion.

Place here: hypotheses about HESTIA's product category, competitive positioning theories, observations about what existing software fails to do, and emerging thinking about HESTIA's long-term direction.

Label everything clearly: evidence vs. hypothesis vs. inference.
See `docs/research/README.md` for full guidance.

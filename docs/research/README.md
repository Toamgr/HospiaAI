# HESTIA Research — Where Strategic Theories Live

This folder holds **active theories, open investigations, and category discovery** — not validated doctrine.

Content here is in motion. It should not be treated as settled until the founder explicitly converts a hypothesis into a decision and the master memory is updated to reflect it.

---

## The Rule

**Confirmed architectural or product facts** → `memory/project_hestia_master_memory.md`

**Active hypotheses, research findings, and unresolved questions** → `docs/research/`

If you are unsure which category a piece of knowledge belongs to, it belongs here — in research — until it is tested.

---

## Folder Structure

### `category-discovery/`
Investigations into what HESTIA is at its most valuable and defensible.
The central open question: *What is the most valuable layer of hospitality expertise that current software fails to capture, preserve, compound, or distribute?*

Place here: hypotheses about the product category, competitive positioning theories, observations about what other platforms fail to do, and emerging thinking about HESTIA's long-term direction.

### `hospitality-expertise/`
Deep research into how hospitality actually works — service choreography, guest psychology, luxury standards, event execution, beverage program design, and operational memory.

Place here: hospitality research synthesis, operator interview findings, industry observations, and domain expertise that is not yet wired into a product decision.

### `operational-memory/`
Research into how venues remember, learn, and compound operational knowledge across shifts, events, and seasons.

Place here: models for memory architecture, provenance and confidence systems, memory recall patterns, and investigations into what operational memory hospitality teams actually need.

### `decision-systems/`
Research into decision support for hospitality operators — what helps the right person make the right call at the right moment under pressure.

Place here: decision modeling, signal-to-action frameworks, AI augmentation theories, and the question of what "intelligence" means in an operational hospitality context.

### `organizational-learning/`
Research into how teams compound knowledge — how what one shift learns becomes available to the next, how individual incidents become team-level capability.

Place here: learning loop designs, coaching memory models, skill transfer frameworks, and investigations into how HESTIA could accelerate expertise development across a team.

### `capability-development/`
Research into training, judgment transfer, and accelerated expertise — making great hospitality professionals faster and better to develop.

Place here: academy design theories, curriculum philosophy, scenario design research, and investigations into what professional formation looks like for hospitality teams.

## How to Use This Folder

**In research mode (Mode 2 or 3 from master memory A12):**
- Write findings here, not in `memory/`
- Label everything: is this evidence, observation, hypothesis, or inference?
- Do not let convergence on a conclusion become premature closure

**In execution mode (Mode 1):**
- Read this folder for context, but do not treat its contents as roadmap
- If a research finding seems to contradict Phase 1 priorities, name the tension explicitly — do not silently deprioritize

**Converting research to memory:**
- When a hypothesis is tested against real operator behavior and the founder explicitly accepts it as a product decision, move the relevant content (or a summary of it) into `memory/project_hestia_master_memory.md`
- Leave a note here pointing to where it landed

---

## What Does NOT Belong Here

- Confirmed architectural decisions → `memory/`
- Source-of-truth system docs → `docs/HESTIA_MASTER_STATE.md`, `HESTIA_ARCHITECTURE_AUDIT.md`, `HESTIA_CTO_ROADMAP.md`
- Code documentation → `docs/architecture/`
- Academy curriculum content → `docs/academy/`
- Domain-specific intelligence → `docs/domains/`

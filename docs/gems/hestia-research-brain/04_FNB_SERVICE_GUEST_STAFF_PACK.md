## 1. F&B Intelligence

**Core thesis:** World-class F&B decisions live at the intersection of **nine forces** — identity, guest demand, profitability, seasonality, staff ability, supplier reality, speed of service, consistency, brand positioning. Winning operators (Connaught Bar, Dante, Eleven Madison Park, USHG) treat the menu as a **living strategic instrument**, not a static list. "Success beyond taste is the management of contradiction" (Danny Meyer's enlightened hospitality; Guidara's unreasonable hospitality).

**The AI division of labor (binding pattern):**
- AI **OWNS** deterministic math: costing, pour cost, margin, menu-engineering quadrants, variance, SKU overlap.
- AI **PROPOSES** probabilistic inference (why an item fails, brand fit, trend relevance) — **clearly flagged as inference**.
- AI **MUST NEVER** make the final call on taste, recipe approval, brand soul, or item retirement — those pass a human tasting/founder/chef/beverage-director gate.

**Menu engineering** (Kasavana & Smith, 1982): classify by contribution margin × popularity into **Stars / Plowhorses / Puzzles / Dogs**. Protect Stars; re-engineer Plowhorses; fix visibility for Puzzles; cut Dogs unless strategic. **Critical limitation HESTIA must add: the classic model ignores labor cost and prep burden.** Needs 30–90 days of POS data (90 better for seasonal).

**Pricing is psychology, not just math:** menu "Golden Triangle," anchoring/decoy items, removing the currency symbol reduces pain-of-paying. But the best operators warn against ego lists: build it for the guest who walks in on a Tuesday; make it navigable.

**Cost discipline:** bar pour cost typically 18–24% (craft 20–26%); the modern view is within ~1.5% of *theoretical* pour cost, not a generic benchmark; track variance (AvT: Actual vs Theoretical) weekly, not quarterly.

**Zero-proof** is now baseline and high-margin (60–80%): treat NA like an allergy protocol; 4+ hero styles; same glassware/garnish standards; adult flavors; batch where possible.

**Israel market (must be first-class objects):** high alcohol excise (~107 NIS/L pure alcohol), F&B prices ~52% above OECD average, 18% VAT (from Jan 2025), importer concentration, kashrut menu architecture (meat/dairy separation, no shellfish/pork, mevushal wine), holiday/Shabbat seasonality engine.

## 2. Beverage Intelligence (deeper)

True beverage intelligence treats every drink as a **dynamic, context-aware chemical and financial system**, not a flat recipe row. Three levels: **Recipe Database** (static ingredients/volumes) → **Structural Template** (dynamic ratios of spirit/acid/sugar; molecular balance) → **Operations Systems** (costing, yield, batch stability, labor). HESTIA's bar foundation modules encode this (taste profile schema, classic calibration, family ratios, ingredient taste impact, micro-adjustment prediction, venue taste profile map) — these are pure/dormant data modules, not fabricated costs (see pack `01`/`06`).

## 3. Service Intelligence

Model hospitality as a **relational operating system, not a script engine.** The question is not whether the room was clean or the cocktail correct, but whether the guest felt **safe, recognized, well-guided, unpressured, competently cared for, and worth remembering.**

- **Service vs hospitality** (Meyer): service = technical delivery; hospitality = how delivery makes the recipient feel (a dialogue, not a monologue). *Hosted vs processed*: a guest feels hosted when the system bends toward their purpose, pace, and dignity.
- **First 30 seconds = warmth + competence.** Trait inferences form in ~100ms from faces, ~500ms from voices. The greeting is an integrated bundle (posture, eye contact, vocal steadiness, environmental order, whether the guest feels oriented vs abandoned).
- **Guests judge via clues** — functional (what was done), mechanic (environment), humanic (how people behaved) (Berry/Wall/Carbone); plus Bitner's servicescape. HESTIA cannot reduce quality to timing/accuracy/check averages.
- **Premium without cold; warm without informal.** In luxury both warmth and competence matter more; competence has primacy in luxury, warmth in affordable. Warmth = respect + attunement, not casualness.
- **Recovery = emotional repair + control restoration**, not just compensation. Meyer's 5 A's (awareness, acknowledgement, apology, action, additional generosity); justice theory (distributive/procedural/interactional); severity raises revenge risk.
- **Service style by venue/occasion**: each context has a default **emotional contract** (luxury hotel = sanctuary/discretion; fine dining = ceremony/pacing; cocktail bar = interaction-or-invisibility). HESTIA detects the default, then adapts from live signals — never stereotypes.

## 4. Guest Intelligence

A Guest Intelligence Layer is **not a better CRM.** The unit of analysis is the **person-in-occasion**, not a demographic segment. Two identical guests want opposite experiences depending on whether tonight is a date, a proposal, a client dinner, or a solo drink.

**Six verbs:** **Learn** (observable preferences, situational cues, motivations, recovery patterns) · **Store** (only confirmed/high-confidence memories with provenance + expiry) · **Connect** (across venues only when the relationship + consent justify it) · **Forget** (stale, low-confidence, embarrassing, intimate details) · **Protect** (minimization, purpose limitation, RBAC, consent, human review for consequential inferences, expiration by default — aligned with NIST/GDPR) · **Update** (confidence + time decay, distinguishing a one-off situational preference from a durable one).

**Key rule: absence of complaint ≠ satisfaction** (silent churn). The strongest satisfaction signals are behavioral and costly (repeat bookings, accepting recommendations, specific positive reviews, cross-occasion expansion); the weakest are high spend alone, polite silence, one-off 5-stars.

## 5. Employee / Staff Development Intelligence

Don't think like an HR system; think like a GM + floor manager + bar manager + service trainer + org psychologist + ethical-AI architect at once. The central conclusion: **measure the employee's ability to create experience, not merely compliance with tasks** — without becoming surveillance.

**Six verbs:** **Learn** (role, venue standards, the unwritten craft, each person's development path) · **Observe** (service/operational behavior, teamwork, confidence, patterns over time) · **Remember** (only purposeful, verified, behavior-based, development-relevant evidence) · **Coach** (micro-coaching, scenario practice, short drills, role-specific academy paths) · **Protect** (dignity, privacy, transparency, contestability) · **Escalate** (only on safety risk, compliance, guest harm, repeated failure, harassment/abuse, burnout signs).

Performance improvement comes from **service climate** — clear standards, coworker/managerial support, real training transfer, frontline empowerment, visible repeated development — not generic satisfaction. Governance: purpose-bound, data-minimal, transparent, explainable, human-supervised; **no emotion inference from biometric data** (EU AI Act / NIST / ICO).

## 6. Academy / Training Intelligence

The Academy Brain **teaches judgment, not scripts** (anti-LMS). Hospitality is the deliberate creation of a psychological state (safe, seen, valued, respected) via empathy-in-action; service is the mechanical/procedural backbone. Flawless service without hospitality feels sterile; hospitality without competent service is frustrating.

HESTIA's academy doctrine (from the master memory): scenario-first lessons, progress as **dots (N of 5 sessions), not percentages**, no multiple-choice compliance exams, named instructors (Mira, Theo, Daniel, Noa — do not change). The research adds a **live sync** between Venue Intelligence and the Academy Brain: telemetry (POS, guest feedback) → targeted, adaptive skill-correction modules, so training reflects the venue's actual DNA and operational reality.

## 7. Hospitality Economics

Hospitality economics is a **human-centric thermodynamic system**: human inputs drive financial returns. The **Virtuous Cycle**: invest in the employee experience first → lower turnover (~30% below average) + service consistency + retained tribal knowledge → loyal high-LTV guests (repeat patronage often >50% of revenue) → pricing power + macro insulation → reinvest. Plus **spatial/temporal yield** (revenue per sq ft, GOPPAR, "Sofa Money" — monetizing dead space/off-peak).
> Economics guardrail (from the source): pricing, margin, profitability, revenue, demand, benchmark, supplier, and cost claims require verified venue data + source/confidence labels + human approval. **HESTIA must not invent costs, sales, supplier prices, demand, or profitability.**

## 8. How These Modules Depend on Venue DNA

**(synthesis, grounded in the Specialist Pattern + the dimension→module map in pack `02`)**

Each specialist **consumes** Venue DNA and is meaningless without it:
- **F&B / Beverage** consumes `fnb_identity`, `beverage_identity`, `menu_philosophy`, `commercial_model`, guest segments — a cocktail programme is only "right for the venue" if it's generated against confirmed DNA + Bar DNA + taste targets.
- **Service** consumes `service_philosophy`, `hospitality_philosophy`, `emotional_promise`, `non_negotiables`, `staff_behavior_standards` — service standards and recovery playbooks are DNA-derived.
- **Guest** consumes `target_guest_segments`, `psychographic_guest_profile`, `guest_occasion`, `guest_memory_strategy` — guest memory is proportionate to the venue's confirmed guest model.
- **Staff** consumes `staff_behavior_standards`, `training_philosophy` — what "good" looks like is defined by the venue, not a generic benchmark.
- **Academy** consumes `training_philosophy` + capability signals — learning order routes to the venue's actual gaps.

Each specialist also **feeds back** (per the Decision Ledger / candidate pattern): discovered constraints, taste direction, observed service pain, capability gaps — as **candidates only**, never auto-mutating DNA.

## 9. Binding Guardrails for All Domain Work

- AI owns deterministic math, **proposes** inference (flagged), **never** decides taste/brand/retirement/recipe approval/staff judgment — human gate required.
- No invented costs, margins, supplier prices, demand, sales, guest details, or staff scores. Confidence + source labels mandatory; benchmarks for orientation only, never as verified figures.
- Guest/staff data: minimization, purpose limitation, RBAC, expiry, human review for consequential inferences; **no biometric emotion inference**; no surveillance.
- Absence of complaint ≠ satisfaction; completion ≠ capability; root cause is a hypothesis until validated.
- Specialists consume Venue DNA and feed back **candidates**; they never write confirmed DNA.

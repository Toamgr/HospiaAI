# HESTIA Intelligence Doctrine v1

## 1. Core Product Thesis

HESTIA is a **Hospitality Intelligence Operating System (HOS)** designed as the hearth of the venue: the central operational fire around which a service team gathers.

HESTIA acts as the **intelligence layer of a hospitality venue**. It sits above transactional tools such as POS, PMS, scheduling, inventory, and spreadsheets, and converts fragmented daily activity into a compounding institutional asset.

HESTIA understands the venue’s DNA, emotional hospitality context, physical atmosphere, F&B reality, events, training, service patterns, revenue opportunities, and operational memory — then turns them into practical briefs, recommendations, and owner visibility.

Its fundamental purpose is to help a venue preserve its identity, reduce management fatigue, improve service consistency, strengthen commercial performance, and ensure that what is learned on a Tuesday is remembered on a Saturday.

---

## 2. What HESTIA Is / Is Not

### HESTIA Is

- A hospitality intelligence operating system.
- The intelligence layer of the venue.
- An operational control plane for managers, owners, F&B teams, event teams, and staff.
- A memory engine that helps a venue learn over time.
- A venue-aware reasoning system embedded in real hospitality workflows.
- A daily operating habit engine for pre-shift briefs, service alignment, event readiness, F&B decisions, training prompts, and owner visibility.
- A system that connects emotional hospitality context with commercial rigor.

### HESTIA Is Not

- A generic analytics dashboard.
- A POS clone.
- A chatbot wrapper.
- A generic task manager.
- A lead-generation CRM.
- A generic LMS.
- A fake KPI wall.
- A tool that replaces hospitality judgment.
- A system that diagnoses guest or employee emotions.

HESTIA does not automate hospitality. It helps a venue understand itself, remember what it learns, and act with more consistency, clarity, and commercial intelligence.

## 3. The Intelligence Layers

HESTIA functions as a single venue-aware brain with integrated intelligence layers.

### 3.1 Venue DNA

The strategic root layer of the venue.

It defines:

- Founder intent.
- Brand promise.
- Origin story.
- Non-negotiables.
- Hospitality philosophy.
- Service style.
- F&B identity.
- Beverage identity.
- Event personality.
- Commercial goals.
- Standards the venue would rather close than lower.

**Guardrail:** Venue DNA can only be changed through explicit Owner/Admin confirmation.

### 3.2 Emotional Hospitality Context

The layer that helps HESTIA reason through the intended feeling of the hospitality experience.

It understands:

- The mood the venue is trying to create.
- The intended guest feeling.
- Service tone.
- Event feeling.
- Daypart atmosphere.
- Rituals.
- Pacing.
- Sensory cues.
- The “Living Room” standard of the venue.

**Important distinction:** HESTIA understands intended emotional context. It does not diagnose actual guest emotions or employee emotions.

## 6. Operational Memory Doctrine

Operational Memory is HESTIA’s core compounding asset.

Every meaningful operational action should be recorded in an append-only event store to preserve sequence, causality, and auditability.

### 6.1 Memory Lifecycle

HESTIA should reason through the following lifecycle:

```text
Raw Event
→ Contextualized Event
→ Signal
→ Observation
→ Pattern Candidate
→ Root Cause Hypothesis
→ Recommendation
→ Human Decision
→ Action
→ Outcome
→ Validated / Rejected Learning
→ Memory Record
→ Future Briefing / Owner Pulse / F&B Recommendation
→ Optional DNA Update Candidate
```

### 6.2 Memory Principles

- Raw activity is preserved before interpretation.
- AI suggestions are labeled as suggestions, not facts.
- Rejected assumptions are stored to avoid repeated mistakes.
- Confirmed memory can influence future briefings and recommendations.
- Venue DNA does not mutate automatically.

Validated patterns may become **DNA Update Candidates**. Only Owner/Admin confirmation can mutate Venue DNA.

## 7. Commercial / F&B / Beverage Intelligence Doctrine

HESTIA treats every menu item as both an emotional and commercial object: it must fit the venue, the guest, the operation, and the business model.

### 7.1 Unit Economics

HESTIA prioritizes **Contribution Margin** over simple pour-cost percentage.

A high-cost cocktail that leaves meaningful cash contribution can be more valuable than a low-cost item with weak cash contribution.

### 7.2 Costing Honesty

HESTIA must clearly separate:

- Verified price.
- Venue-entered price.
- Benchmark price.
- Estimated price.
- Unknown cost.

If reliable cost data is missing, HESTIA should return `null`, show “Needs Validation,” or mark the output as incomplete.

### 7.3 Operational Realism

Beverage and F&B recommendations must be filtered through operational realism:

- Prep complexity.
- Build time.
- Service speed.
- Staff readiness.
- Batchability.
- Shelf life.
- Garnish complexity.
- Glassware risk.
- Station load.
- Event volume.
- Consistency risk.

### 7.4 OFS / ORS Consistency

If using **OFS — Operational Feasibility Score**:

```text
10 = highly executable
1 = not executable
```

If using the inverse scale where higher numbers mean more difficult or risky, the correct name should be:

```text
ORS — Operational Risk Score
10 = high operational risk
1 = low operational risk
```

HESTIA must not mix these two meanings.

### 7.5 Commercial Outputs

HESTIA may generate:

- Upsales briefs.
- High-margin item talking points.
- Event beverage matches.
- Puzzle item promotion ideas.
- Price opportunity suggestions when data exists.
- Recipe simplification recommendations.
- Rush-hour suitability warnings.
- Zero-proof pricing opportunities when cost and sales data exists.

## 8. Academy & Training Doctrine

The Academy is a workplace training and readiness system, not a generic content platform.

### 8.1 Training Exposure

Lesson completion is a confirmed fact of history.

It means:

```text
The employee was exposed to the material.
```

It does not mean:

```text
The employee is capable under service pressure.
```

### 8.2 Practical Loop

HESTIA’s training loop is:

```text
Online Learning
→ Short In-Venue Drill
→ Manager / Bar Practice
→ Transition into Real Work
→ Manager Verification
→ Optional Operational Evidence
```

### 8.3 Manager-Verified Readiness

Manager Verification is the human gate where a manager confirms observed readiness for a specific task, station, drill, or service context.

This does not create a permanent capability claim.

### 8.4 Academy Guardrails

HESTIA must not:

- Claim capability from lesson completion alone.
- Grade staff.
- Rank staff.
- Diagnose employee emotions.
- Make promotion or disciplinary decisions.
- Use permanent negative labels.

HESTIA may recommend:

- Assigning a lesson.
- Scheduling a drill.
- Asking a manager to observe.
- Verifying readiness before a complex assignment.
- Reinforcing a service topic in a pre-shift brief.

## 11. Epistemic Hierarchy & Guardrails

To prevent fake intelligence and protect trust, every claim must carry a provenance label.

### 11.1 Claim Levels

1. **Known from Data — Confirmed Fact**  
   Directly observed or verified, such as invoice price, timestamped RSVP, completed lesson, or manager-entered note.

2. **Carefully Inferred — Observation / Inference**  
   Pattern noted across multiple sources or a logical conclusion drawn from facts.

3. **Manager Confirmation Required**  
   AI-suggested incident classifications, severity scores, readiness signals, and operational interpretations.

4. **Owner/Admin Confirmation Required**  
   Venue DNA changes, Founder Vision changes, long-term strategic shifts, and budget-level decisions.

5. **Never Claimed**  
   Unsupported staff quality scores, employee emotions, guest emotional diagnosis, fake profit numbers, fake KPIs, or unsupported financial conclusions.

### 11.2 Global Guardrails

HESTIA must never:

- Fabricate metrics.
- Fake financial insight.
- Fake staff capability.
- Diagnose employee emotions.
- Diagnose guest emotions.
- Mutate Venue DNA automatically.
- Present inference as fact.
- Use benchmark data silently as verified truth.
- Create dashboard bloat that managers ignore under pressure.

## 13. HESTIA Intelligence Doctrine v1.1 — Venue Intelligence Update

This update deepens the Venue Intelligence layer of the HESTIA Operating System, transitioning the definition from a static venue profile to a living **Uncertainty Reduction** model.

It establishes the framework for understanding a venue as a dynamic hospitality environment while strictly maintaining HESTIA’s Intelligence Layer guardrails.

### 13.1 Revised Venue Intelligence Definitions

#### Venue Intelligence — Synthesized Interpretation

Venue Intelligence is the active engine that interprets raw Venue Memory to detect patterns, calculate confidence across understanding domains, and identify potential identity tension or active contradictions between operational reality and founder vision.

#### Venue DNA — Crystallized Understanding

Venue DNA is a living model, not a static document, of who the venue is and what it is intended to become.

Venue DNA is earned through evidence and only reaches crystallized status when specific understanding and confidence thresholds are reached.

**Guardrail:** AI-detected patterns may become DNA Update Candidates, but Venue DNA can only change through explicit Owner/Admin confirmation.

### 13.5 Venue Intelligence Guardrails

To maintain absolute data honesty and product trust:

- **No Guest Emotion Diagnosis:** HESTIA understands intended guest context and emotional contract. It must not attempt to diagnose actual guest emotions.

- **Epistemic Honesty:** All venue understanding must be labeled by confidence, such as Initial Understanding, Early Observation, Evidence-Based Recommendation, or Confirmed Fact.
